About Smart Grid

A smart grid is an electricity network that uses digital technology to watch and control the flow of power. It finds faults quickly, reroutes electricity, and helps use more solar and wind energy.

GridPulse Systems has modernized over 15,000 miles of transmission lines and integrated 8.5 gigawatts of solar and wind power.

Battery storage keeps extra energy from solar panels so it can be used at night.

The SCADA control centre watches the whole grid all the time.
